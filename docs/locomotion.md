# Locomotion

- **Wheels first:** a low, wide rolling base is the most stable and easiest to build.
- **Treads as a swap:** the same hip mount takes a tread module for rugs, thresholds and yards.
- **Legs later:** possible as a module, but harder to build and to keep upright.
- **Staying upright around a large dog:** low center of gravity (batteries at the bottom), a base wider
  than the body, a tilt sensor so it knows it has been knocked over, and self-righting later.
- **Soft cover:** fabric over the body; nothing that looks like a chew toy; no dangling cables.
